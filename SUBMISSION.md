**Title:** Relativity Juice: Neon Racer (Procedural 70s/80s Synthwave Supercar, 3D Particle Exhaust & 124 BPM Soundtrack in Blender Python + Geometry Nodes)

**Entrant / Team Name:** Chris

**Interpretation of Speed (What It Is):**
Speed is felt through relative motion, camera choreography, and rhythm. In this 29.5-second procedural Blender animation (25-second 600-frame race plus Blender-rendered title and end cards), a 1970s/80s wedge supercar (`HeroRacer`, metallic gunmetal with cyan neon piping) races a crimson rival interceptor (`RivalRacer`) down a wet-mirror neon highway. Across five beat-synced native Blender camera cuts (low wheel launch cam with 3D particle exhaust sparks, sweeping helicopter orbit cam, front-3/4 door-to-door overtake cam, wide-FOV hyper-boost chase cam, and a high top-down overview finale), the cars weave, overtake, and ignite hyper-drive. In the final top-overview shot (`CamOverview`), the GTA-style dual speed-bar dashboard in the bottom-right corner (`L-CAR` left bar, `R-CAR` right bar) maxes out at `999 KM/H · MAX!` as both cars rocket down the highway and disappear into the segmented sunset sun.

**Workflow Notes (Best Procedural & Programmatic Animation / Best Motion Design / Best Simulation & FX):**
Nothing is hand-modelled, hand-keyed, or hand-painted. Running `bun run racer` and `bun run media:racer` (or `bun run blend` to produce `out/neon-racer.blend`) builds the entire 600-frame scene from Python (`src/racer.py`, `src/synthwave.py`):
- **Procedural Wedge Supercars & Wheel Rigs (`src/racer.py`):** Both 70s wedge supercars (`HeroRacer` and `RivalRacer`) are generated from procedural quad profiles with metallic clearcoat `Principled BSDF` paint, tinted canopy glass, emissive neon edge piping, 5 rear window louvers, rear spoilers, full-width 80s grid lightbars that flash on beats 2 and 4 of the 124 BPM snare (`snare_on`), a rear `RENDERJUICE` bumper plate on `HeroRacer`, dual plasma thrusters, and 4 child wheel objects per car. Wheel rotation (`rotation_euler.x = -distance / radius`) and lateral S-curve chassis roll/pitch banking are keyframed mathematically on Blender F-curves at every frame with Cycles 3D motion blur (`shutter = 0.35`).
- **Geometry Nodes 3D Particle Exhaust Sparks & Mountain Waves (`TrailNodes` & `HighwayNodes`):** Two `GeometryNodeTree` modifiers are constructed node-by-node in Python: `TrailNodes` advects 64 emitter points per car through a turbulent 3D spray cone (`seed_phase`, `spray_vel`), computes per-particle `heat`, and instances stretched 3D icosphere sparks (`GeometryNodeInstanceOnPoints` + `GeometryNodeMeshIcoSphere`) shaded from white-hot cyan/gold at the nozzles to neon magenta/orange at the tail. `HighwayNodes` pulses the flanking wireframe mountain ridges at 124 BPM, paired with an expanding `BoostShockwave` ring at frame 253.
- **5-Camera Timeline Editing & Analog Corner Speedometers:** Five Blender cameras (`CamLaunch`, `CamHeli`, `CamOvertake`, `CamBoost`, `CamOverview`) are bound to `scene.timeline_markers` at frames `1, 85, 169, 253, 421` with shutter-safe guard keyframes. Two background-free analog speedometer dials (`RacerHUD_Rig`) sit in the bottom-left (`L-CAR`) and bottom-right (`R-CAR`) corners with keyframed red needles that sweep into the redline and hit `999 KM/H · MAX!` in Shot 5, rendered 100% natively inside Cycles.
- **Programmatic 124 BPM Stereo 90s Synthwave Soundtrack (`src/synthwave.py`):** Synthesized in pure Python at 48 kHz stereo across the full 29.5 seconds (analog pitch-swept kick, gated-reverb snare, 16th-note hi-hats, stereo Simmons tom fills synced to all 4 camera cuts at `3.5 s, 7.0 s, 10.5 s, 17.5 s`, sidechained 16th-note sawtooth arp bass over `Am - F - C - G`, stereo chorus polysynth pads, dotted-eighth delayed lead melody with fifth harmony in the overdrive finale, and a speed-synced turbo plasma whine).
